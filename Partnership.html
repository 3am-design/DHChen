Partnership

Who we work with, and how

A great deal of what we do is introduction. Putting two organisations in a room because between them they have the whole answer is often worth more than either could have done with the money alone.

How we partner

Alongside, not above

We back our partners’ judgement about their own field ahead of our own. That means multi-year commitments rather than annual ones, unrestricted funding where the work calls for it, and a say in design rather than only in reporting.

It also means staying through the difficult stretches. A partnership that only holds while a programme is going well is not a partnership; it is a grant with a longer cover letter.

What we look for

Four things, in this order

  1. 01

    A shared measure of success

    We agree at the start what would count as the work having mattered, and we agree it together rather than imposing it.

  2. 02

    Something we can add beyond money

    Convening, expertise, shared services, or an introduction that would not otherwise have happened.

  3. 03

    A path to standing on its own

    We ask what would still be running if we stopped funding, and we design backwards from the answer.

  4. 04

    Willingness to say what went wrong

    The learning we publish is only worth reading if it includes the parts that did not work.
