Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data

Last updated March 2026

This statement explains how The D. H. Chen Foundation and its related entities (“we”, “us”, “our” or the “Foundation”) manage personal information under Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.

It sets out how personal data — any information from which an individual can be identified — is collected, used, protected and retained. By providing personal data to the Foundation, you accept the terms of this statement.

On this page

  • Data collection, purpose and use
  • Links to other websites
  • Use of cookies
  • Who may process your personal data
  • Retention
  • Protection

Data collection, purpose and use

The Foundation collects personal data directly from individuals, and indirectly through third parties, for the following purposes:

  • Funding and scholarship applications — assessment and vetting
  • Programmes and events — implementation and hosting
  • Personnel records — recruitment and human resources management
  • Administrative and operational purposes

What we collect depends on how you interact with us. Some collection is necessary for the website to work or for an event to be provided. Marketing communications are sent only with your consent, or where the law otherwise permits.

Links to other websites

This website links to sites operated by others. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content or terms, and you should review those separately before providing any personal data to them.

Use of cookies

This website uses cookies to improve the experience of using it. Continuing to use the site indicates that you accept them. Declining cookies may limit some functionality.

Who may process your personal data

Your personal data may be accessible to:

  • Employees of the Foundation
  • IT and marketing service providers acting on our instructions
  • Providers supporting our events and activities
  • Regulators and law enforcement, where we are legally obliged to disclose

Where a third party processes personal data on our behalf, we require it to comply with this statement.

Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, and is erased once that purpose has been met.

Protection

We take practicable steps to prevent unauthorised access to, or modification or disclosure of, personal data. Access by employees is limited to those who need it for their work.

Access and correction

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal data by writing to the Chief Operating Officer, The D. H. Chen Foundation, 25–26/F, Nan Fung Tower, 88 Connaught Road Central, Central, Hong Kong, or by email to info@dhchenfoundation.com. We will verify your identity before acting on a request, and a fee may be charged.

Changes to this statement

We may revise this statement from time to time. The version published on this page at the time your personal data is processed is the one that applies.
