What We Fund

A Partnership for Meaningful Change

We believe meaningful change begins with people and grows through collaboration.

As a philanthropic trust, we support initiatives, including early-stage ideas, that nurture human potential, strengthen communities, and help shape a more compassionate society. We see our role as more than a funder. Through trusted partnerships, learning and capacity building, and the sharing of insights, we help cultivate promising ideas, accompanying them on their journey from possibility to sustainable impact.

Our funding approach is guided by our commitment to:

  • Engage with Compassion and Understanding
  • Invest in Human Potential
  • Connect the Dots
  • Seed New Possibilities
  • Cultivate Shared Learning
Our funding approach

Funding & Partnerships

We provide financial and partnership support to organisations whose work aligns with our priorities and values.

We are particularly interested in initiatives that contribute to:

  1. 01

    Cultivating Purpose and Potential

    Supporting people to discover purpose, develop their strengths, and realise their potential. We are interested in initiatives that foster character, lifelong learning, personal growth, leadership, and the capacity to contribute meaningfully to society.

  2. 02

    Supporting Wellbeing Across Life

    Supporting holistic approaches to wellbeing throughout the different stages of life. This includes mental, emotional, physical, social, and spiritual wellbeing, as well as initiatives that help people navigate life's opportunities, transitions, and challenges with resilience and dignity.

  3. 03

    Strengthening Meaningful Connections

    Supporting efforts that nurture relationships, strengthen communities, and foster a sense of belonging. We are interested in initiatives that bring people together, build mutual understanding, encourage care for one another, and create the conditions for individuals and communities to thrive.

  4. 04

    Enabling Positive Social Change

    Supporting initiatives that address emerging social needs, strengthen the social good ecosystem, and contribute to a more caring, connected, and compassionate society. This may include social innovation, cross-sector collaboration, organisational capacity building, knowledge sharing, and efforts that help create lasting and systemic change.

Foundation-led Strategic Initiatives

Alongside our funding to values-aligned partners, we actively develop and support initiatives that explore emerging opportunities and strengthen the wider social ecosystem.

Through platforms such as D'Hub and D'Ventures, we bring together partners, practitioners, innovators and changemakers to:

  • Explore new possibilities and emerging social needs
  • Pilot innovative approaches and solutions
  • Strengthen organisational and sector capacity
  • Foster strategic partnerships and cross-sector collaboration
  • Generate learning that can benefit the wider community

These initiatives reflect our belief that lasting change often requires more than funding alone. By convening people, building relationships and creating space for experimentation, we can help ideas grow into meaningful and sustainable impact.

Beyond Traditional Boundaries

Meaningful change can come from many places.

We are interested in exploring opportunities with businesses, social enterprises, and other organisations that are leveraging the power of private enterprise to contribute to the public good.

If you are developing innovative approaches that align with our values and contribute to human potential, stronger communities, or positive social change, we would welcome a conversation.

Our Funding Considerations

To ensure our resources are directed towards achieving meaningful and sustainable social impact, we generally do not support:

  • Politically oriented initiatives
  • Individual funding requests, except through Foundation-established scholarships or under exceptional circumstances
  • Programmes that are scheduled to commence within three months of the application submission date
