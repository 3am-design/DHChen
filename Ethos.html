Our Ethos

Our origin, our purpose, and the values we hold

We are a Hong Kong family philanthropic trust, putting compassion into action with partners to address evolving social needs.

Our Origin

A family trust, and what it was set up to do

The D. H. Chen Foundation advances compassion-based philanthropy through initiatives, partnerships and sector knowledge that respond to evolving social needs. Rather than funding alone, we work alongside the organisations and individuals we support — contributing time, expertise and networks as readily as capital.

That work runs through five institutes — Education Excellence, Impact Strategy, Mindful Living, Wellbeing, and the Above and Beyond Institute — each carrying the same philosophy into a different corner of the sector.

Our Purpose

True compassion springs from selfless wisdom.

Through education and co-creating philanthropic initiatives with like-minded partners, we strive to cultivate individual’s virtues and mindfulness, enhance the wellbeing of individual & community, and sustain admirable efforts.

How that purpose is pursued — the five moves we make, and the order we make them in — is set out under Approach.

Our approach

Values

Five principles we test ourselves against first

A compassionate society is built on five principles. They are the test we apply to our own conduct before we apply it to anything else.

  • 01

    Gratitude

    Recognising what we have been given, and giving it onward.

  • 02

    Trust

    Backing our partners’ judgement, and holding to our own word.

  • 03

    Openness

    Listening first, and staying willing to be changed by what we hear.

  • 04

    Respect

    Meeting every person and organisation as an equal.

  • 05

    Selflessness

    Putting the work, and the people it serves, ahead of the credit.

Creating a compassionate society together through approaches that are impact-driven, sustainable and innovative — societal improvement, not commercial success, is the measure we hold ourselves to.
