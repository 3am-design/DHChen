How We Put Compassion into Action

At The D. H. Chen Foundation, we believe our contribution goes beyond funding.

Guided by our Founder Dr. Din Hwa Chen's belief to "Care for others as well as you would care for yourself", we bring together different forms of capital, including financial resources, knowledge, relationships, experience and opportunities, to support positive change. We see philanthropy not as a transaction, but as a journey of learning, partnership and shared growth.

Through five interconnected practices, we seek to put compassion into action and contribute to a more compassionate society.

Meaningful change begins with understanding.

We take time to listen deeply and build trusted relationships with the people, communities and organisations we engage. By approaching others with openness, curiosity and humility, we develop a richer understanding of different experiences, perspectives and aspirations.

This ongoing process of listening and learning helps shape how we act, partner and grow together.

We believe everyone has the capacity to learn, contribute and flourish.

We invest in people, leadership, learning and organisational development, recognising that meaningful change is sustained by individuals and communities who continue to evolve and adapt. Through shared learning and mutual support, we seek to cultivate the conditions in which potential can be realised and aspirations can take shape.

We believe our greatest contribution often lies in the connections we help create.

Alongside financial support, we bring intellectual, social and relational capital by connecting people, ideas, organisations and opportunities. By sharing knowledge, building relationships and linking complementary strengths, we help unlock possibilities that may not emerge in isolation.

Because when the right connections are made, new pathways for impact can emerge.

We remain open to what could be.

We seek out emerging needs, fresh perspectives and promising ideas that can contribute to a more compassionate society. Through experimentation, learning and thoughtful risk-taking, we create space for new possibilities to be explored, refined and realised.

We believe innovation is not only about finding solutions, but also about expanding what people imagine to be possible.

We believe everyone has something to learn and something to contribute. Through reflection, dialogue, experimentation and knowledge exchange, we learn alongside our partners and communities. These insights not only strengthen individual initiatives, but also contribute to the wider social good sector.
