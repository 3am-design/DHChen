Building a compassionate society through values, partnerships & action.

The D. H. Chen Foundation advances compassion-based philanthropy through initiatives, partnerships and sector knowledge that respond to evolving social needs.

We are a Hong Kong family philanthropic trust, putting compassion into action with partners to address evolving social needs.

Being

This is where awareness begins.

We connect with our authentic selves to become more aware of the possibilities and opportunities that can inspire meaningful change.

About knowing who we are, what we care about deeply, and how we make our most important decisions.

Becoming

This is where possibilities take shape and grow into meaningful contributions.

We connect the self to possibility through exploring potential and purposes, transforming insight into action and nurturing ideas that can create positive social impact.

About what we can be, and what we want to become as individuals and as members of society.

Belonging

This is where individual contributions come together, and where impact becomes collective.

We connect the self to community through stronger social connections, cultivating relationships built on trust, care, and shared purpose.

About who we are, and who we aspire to be, within the broader communities in which we live.

As Visionary Founder, the Foundation launched D’Ventures, a social venture that builds a purpose-driven ecosystem for the local social good community to grow and thrive. By offering Shared Services, a Co-learning Community, and an Integrated Workspace within its D’Hub in the heart of Kowloon, the Foundation brings together the services and space needed to drive sustainable impact and turn compassion into action.

Scholarship

Stemming from a strong belief in the power of education to effect change, the Foundation established and launched the whole new The D. H. Chen Foundation Scholarship Programme (the “Scholarship Programme”) in 2015, a milestone initiative investing in deserving students who will combine their intellect and humanitarian ethos of social service to drive impact for a better tomorrow.
