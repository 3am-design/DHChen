Our Institutes

Where the work lives

Our work runs through five institutes. Each one carries the same philosophy into a different corner of the sector, and each one is answerable for the change it sets out to make.

Education Excellence

Institute of Education Excellence

Investing in teachers, schools and students so that education reaches further than the classroom.

Programmes that treat education as more than attainment — life exploration, compassionate communication, and the confidence to act on what a young person cares about.

  • Education
  • Capacity Building
Discover

Impact Strategy

Institute of Impact Strategy

Turning capital, evidence and networks into strategies the sector can carry on without us.

Emergency support when a shock hits, and the slower work of fellowship, shared learning and measurement that lets a small organisation plan past the current funding cycle.

  • Impact Strategy
  • Catalyst for Collaboration
Discover

Mindful Living

Institute of Mindful Living

Cultivating awareness and compassionate communication in schools, families and workplaces.

Practices that begin with the individual and end up changing how a classroom, a household or a team speaks to itself.

  • Mindful Living
  • Issues-focused
Discover

Wellbeing

Institute of Wellbeing

Supporting the physical and mental health of carers, patients and the communities around them.

Work aimed at the people who hold others up — carers, frontline staff, families — and at the services that too often reach them last.

  • Wellbeing
  • Issues-focused
Discover

Above and Beyond

Above and Beyond Institute

Backing the work that falls between the usual categories, and the people prepared to try it.

Where an idea does not fit an existing pillar but plainly needs doing, this is the institute that takes it on.

  • Capacity Building
  • Issues-focused
Discover

The five institutes are where the work lives; how we go about it is set out under Approach. Everything they publish sits together under Impact.
