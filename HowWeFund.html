1Prepare Your Application

We accept funding applications throughout the year.

We encourage prospective applicants to review our philanthropic approach and priorities before submitting an application form and a concept note to ensure there is a strong alignment between the initiative and the Foundation's areas of focus.

2Submit Application

Complete the appropriate application form

General Applicants

Form A01 (opens in a new tab)

Universities funded by the University Grants Committee (UGC) in Hong Kong

Form U03 (opens in a new tab)

Submit together with a concept note that is clear, thoughtful and authentic

You do not need a polished proposal or a fully developed solution at this stage. Instead, we encourage you to explain:

  • What you hope to do
  • Why it matters
  • Who it aims to serve
  • What you hope to learn along the way

Please keep your concept note to a maximum of 10 pages and include the following:

  1. 01

    Programme Name

  2. 02

    Organisation and Team

    Tell us about your organisation, your mission, and the people leading the work.

  3. 03

    The Need

    What social issue(s) are you seeking to address, and why is it important?

  4. 04

    Programme Objectives, Period and Duration

    What change are you hoping to create and how do you plan to achieve it?

  5. 05

    Implementation Approach and Key Deliverables

    How will the programme be delivered, and what are the key activities and outputs?

  6. 06

    Intended Impact and Outcomes

    What difference do you hope to make, and how will progress be understood and assessed?

  7. 07

    Budget Summary

    Provide a high-level overview of the resources required.

Questions to Guide Your Thinking

There are no "model answers". We value honest reflection, including openness about uncertainties, challenges, and areas where additional support may be needed.

Our goal is not simply to assess programmes, but to understand the people, purpose and possibilities behind them.

The following questions are intended as prompts rather than requirements. You do not need to answer every question individually.

Organisation and Team

  • What is your organisation's vision and mission?
  • How does this initiative contribute to the wider change your organisation seeks to create?
  • What experience, strengths and commitment does your team bring to this work?

The Need

  • What issue are you addressing, who is affected, and why does it matter?
  • How might this initiative contribute to a more compassionate society?
  • What existing services or approaches are already addressing this issue, and what gaps remain?

Programme Objectives

  • What are you seeking to create, strengthen, test or learn?
  • How does the initiative complement or build upon existing efforts?
  • Who are the intended participants or beneficiaries?

Implementation Approach

  • What are the key activities, milestones and deliverables?
  • How will participants and communities be involved in shaping the work?
  • What resources, partnerships or support will be important to successful implementation?

Intended Impact and Outcomes

  • What changes do you hope to see for the people and communities involved?
  • How will you monitor progress and learn from implementation?
  • How might lessons and insights be shared with others?

Supporting Documents

To support our due diligence process, applicants will be asked to provide relevant organisational and financial documents, such as:

  • Registration and incorporation documents (where applicable)
  • Annual reports and annual returns (where applicable)
  • Organisational leadership and governance information
  • Latest audited financial statements
  • Other supporting information relevant to the proposed initiative

Specific requirements vary according to applicant type and will be outlined within the application form.

3Notify after Review within 6 Months from Submission

Every application is reviewed carefully and thoughtfully.

In assessing proposals, we consider factors such as:

  • Alignment with the Foundation's vision, mission and values
  • Potential for meaningful and lasting impact
  • Organisational capacity and readiness
  • Opportunities for learning, innovation and collaboration
  • Sustainability and long-term contribution to the community
  • Potential synergies with existing initiatives and partners

We may contact applicants for additional information during the review process.

If you do not hear from us within six months of submitting your application, you may consider your application unsuccessful.

4Implement Programme

We view funding as the beginning of a partnership rather than the end of a transaction.

During programme implementation, we work closely with our partners through ongoing dialogue, learning, capacity building and knowledge sharing. We value mutual trust and believe that meaningful change is created when organisations, communities and funders learn and grow together. This partnership-oriented approach is also reflected in the Foundation's ongoing engagement, monitoring, reporting, and capacity support to grantees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for funding?

We welcome applications from charitable, educational, community and social purpose organisations whose work aligns with our priorities, values and approach to creating positive social change.

Generally, we provide funding to organisations rather than individuals.

Exceptions may be made under special circumstances or through scholarship programmes established by the Foundation.

Do you provide funding for operating expenses?

We may support operational costs when they are clearly connected to the successful delivery and sustainability of the proposed initiative. Each application is considered on its own merits.

Do you fund pilot projects or innovative ideas?

Yes.

We recognise that meaningful change often requires experimentation and learning. We welcome proposals that test new possibilities, respond to emerging needs, or explore innovative approaches, provided there is a thoughtful rationale, implementation plan and learning strategy.

Do you support multi-year initiatives?

Yes.

We recognise that addressing complex social challenges often requires long-term commitment. Depending on the nature of the initiative, we may consider support beyond a single year.

Do you provide support beyond funding?

Yes.

In addition to financial support, we may offer strategic advice, facilitate connections within our network, share learning opportunities, and help strengthen organisational and sector capacity where appropriate.

If I'm unsure whether my initiative is a good fit, should I still apply?

Yes. We understand that innovative ideas and emerging opportunities do not always fit neatly into predefined categories. If your initiative aligns with our values and contributes to a more compassionate society, we encourage you to submit an application and share your thinking with us.
