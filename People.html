The People Behind Our Shared Journey

Meaningful change begins with people. At The D. H. Chen Foundation, compassion is reflected in how we understand one another, grow and learn alongside others, and build relationships grounded in trust and shared purpose. Our people bring diverse perspectives and experience, united by a commitment to putting compassion into action.

Message from the Board

Compassion has always been at the heart of our Foundation, and it guides every decision we make together.

Since our founding, we have sought to honour Dr. Chen’s belief that we should care for others as well as we would care for ourselves. As a Board, we are grateful to the partners, communities and colleagues who turn that belief into action every day.

Looking ahead, we remain committed to listening carefully, supporting new possibilities and working alongside others towards a more compassionate society.

Biography — Demo profile

Foundation motto

Care for others as well as you would care for yourself.

Meaningful change begins with the time we give to understanding one another. A commitment to listening, learning and working alongside others shapes this approach to philanthropy. Behind every programme are people with their own hopes, concerns and experiences, and those perspectives deserve a place in the decisions that affect their lives.

Collaboration starts by recognising the knowledge already present in a community. The most useful contribution may be a thoughtful question, a practical introduction or the patience to explore an idea together. Working with partners means being open about what is known, honest about what remains uncertain and willing to adjust as new understanding emerges.

This outlook carries into everyday teamwork. Different professional backgrounds make it possible to see a challenge from several directions. Sharing experiences, making space for quieter voices and reflecting on setbacks help build the trust needed to try something new. Progress often comes through small, consistent acts of care, supported by relationships that continue beyond the life of a single project.

The ambition is to help create conditions in which individuals and organisations can grow with confidence. That includes making room for learning, supporting people through change and connecting ideas across boundaries. Compassion becomes practical when it influences how a meeting is held, how a decision is made and how a colleague or community partner is supported. It is an ongoing practice, renewed through the choices made each day.
