Life and Values Education

Our Philanthropic Theme

We support opportunities for people to better understand themselves, discover purpose and navigate life with greater clarity, compassion and resilience.

Learning that helps children and young people understand themselves and others, and grow the values, such as compassion, respect and responsibility, that guide how they live and act.

Opportunities for young people to explore who they are, what matters to them and where they might go, so that the paths they choose in study, work and life carry a sense of purpose.

We believe wellbeing is essential throughout life's journey, from youth to older age.

Support for emotional and psychological wellbeing at every stage of life, from building resilience early on to caring for people when life becomes difficult.

Mindfulness, reflection and other contemplative practices that help people slow down, find calm and meet themselves and others with greater awareness and kindness.

Care that attends to meaning, hope and connection, recognising that wellbeing reaches beyond the body and the mind.

Help for parents, carers and families to build caring, stable relationships, so that every member of the household can thrive.

We support efforts that help people approach ageing and the end of life with dignity, connection and compassion.

Efforts that help older adults stay healthy, connected and engaged, and that change how our communities see and value later life.

Work that helps people, families and carers approach the end of life with dignity, comfort, connection and compassion.

We strengthen the people, organisations, relationships and ecosystems that enable positive change to flourish.

Building the strength of social purpose organisations: their people, leadership, governance and capacity to deliver lasting change.

Backing new ideas and early-stage ventures that take fresh approaches to social needs, from first prototype to sustainable model.

Helping organisations and the wider sector embed impact into how they plan, measure, learn and grow.

Initiatives that help children grow up curious, caring and confident, at school, at home and in their communities.

Initiatives that give young people the space, support and opportunities to discover their values and purpose, and to act on them.

Initiatives that support parents in raising children with warmth, understanding and peace.

Initiatives that help older adults stay healthy, connected and valued, and live later life with dignity.

Initiatives that equip educators to nurture compassion, wellbeing and purpose in the young people they teach.

Initiatives that support social workers and frontline practitioners, and the people they care for.

Initiatives that care for the carers: their wellbeing, their skills and the support around them.

Initiatives that strengthen social purpose organisations and the wider ecosystem they work in.
