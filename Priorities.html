Priorities

Where We Focus Our Efforts

Guided by our compassion-based philanthropic practices, we support initiatives that nurture human potential, strengthen communities and contribute to a more compassionate society.

Our priorities reflect areas where we believe thoughtful action, collaboration and innovation can help create meaningful change.

Priorities: The changes we seek to contribute to

01

Understanding Self and Purpose

Helping people understand themselves, discover purpose and realise their potential.

02

Living Well Through Life

Supporting wellbeing, resilience, ageing and end-of-life experiences with dignity and compassion.

03

Strengthening Communities and Belonging

Fostering caring relationships, families, communities and social cohesion.

04

Enabling Social Good

Supporting the people, organisations and ecosystems that help create a more compassionate society.

01

Deepening Self-Understanding and Purpose

We support opportunities for people to better understand themselves, discover purpose and navigate life with greater clarity, compassion and resilience.

Topics Include:

  • Life and values education
  • Life and career exploration
  • Mindfulness
  • Reflective practices
  • Spiritual wellbeing

02

Nurturing Wellbeing Across Life

We believe wellbeing is essential throughout life's journey, from youth to older age.

Topics Include:

  • Mental health
  • Holistic wellbeing
  • Parenting and family wellbeing
  • Ageing well
  • Dying well

03

Strengthening Communities and Belonging

We support efforts that foster connection, belonging and mutual care among individuals, families and communities.

Topics Include:

  • Community wellbeing
  • Intergenerational connection
  • Compassionate communities
  • Caregiving
  • Social connectedness

04

Enabling Social Good

We strengthen the people, organisations, relationships and ecosystems that enable positive change to flourish.

Topics Include:

  • Social innovation
  • Leadership development
  • Strategic partnerships
  • Sector capacity building
  • Ecosystem development
