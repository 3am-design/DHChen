Future Education in Action 2.0

Fostering values-driven life exploration education through cross-sector collaboration

Institute
Institute of Education Excellence
Running
2022 – ongoing
Partners
14 secondary schools, 3 universities
Download report

A young person is asked to choose a career years before anyone has asked them what they care about. Future Education in Action starts at the other end.

Now in its second phase, the programme brings secondary schools, universities and employers into the same room to design life exploration education built on values rather than on results. Teachers are supported to run it themselves; the Foundation convenes, funds the first three years, and then gets out of the way.

The first phase ran in four schools. What came back from the teachers was not a request for more material but for more time together: the co-design sessions, where a physics teacher, a careers advisor and someone from a partner employer worked out a unit between them, were the part nobody wanted to cut.

We stopped asking them what they want to be.

Phase two widens it: fourteen schools, three universities, and a shared bank of units any of them can take, change and hand back. The Foundation pays for the convening and the evaluation; the schools own the material.

By 2027 the programme should not need us. The measure we set at the start was whether the co-design sessions keep running once the funding stops — not how many students passed through them while it lasted.
